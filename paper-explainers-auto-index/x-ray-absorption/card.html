    Single-shot X-ray absorption spectroscopy with a laser wakefield accelerator

    The X-rays from electrons wiggling in a plasma wave last about 10 femtoseconds and have a smooth, broad spectrum. We used them to record a full absorption spectrum in one shot: first the near-edge structure of titanium, then the extended fine structure of copper, which shows where its neighbouring atoms sit.
