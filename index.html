  • Nature Communications · 2026

    Observation of quantum effects on radiation reaction in strong fields

    Electrons fired head-on into one of the world’s most intense lasers lose energy to the gamma rays they emit. More than 600 collisions showed this energy loss beyond 5σ, and the data favour quantum descriptions over the classical one.

  • Physical Review Letters · 2021

    Temperature equilibration due to charge state fluctuations in dense plasmas

    In a dense plasma, ions are ionized and recombined over and over, so each ion’s charge flickers and its neighbours feel a changing push. We showed that this flicker carries energy between hot electrons and cold ions, and can be as fast as direct collisions.
