    Observation of quantum effects on radiation reaction in strong fields

    Electrons fired head-on into one of the world’s most intense lasers lose energy to the gamma rays they emit. More than 600 collisions showed this energy loss beyond 5σ, and the data favour quantum descriptions over the classical one.
